## State

`7.1.5-sp11-audio-protected` was installed as an isolated first-boot candidate
on the Surface Pro 11 OLED. Its first boot failed before ALSA card
registration because the kernel/module set was incomplete and the entry used
the wrong DTB. Its GRUB entry is disabled and retained only as evidence.

The working `7.1.5-sp11+` kernel, its device tree, initramfs and saved GRUB
entry remain intact. The protected candidate is selected with a one-time
`grub-reboot`; the persistent saved entry remains `sp11-7.1.5-clean`. A failed
or subsequently restarted candidate therefore falls back without changing the
saved default.

## Automatic first-boot capture

`sp11-audio-first-boot.service` is enabled with a command-line condition that
matches only the protected boot entry. It runs a read-only collector after the
sound devices settle and writes timestamped evidence under:

```text
/var/log/sp11-audio-first-boot/
```

It captures the complete boot/kernel journal, audio-specific events, module
state, ALSA controls, SoundWire devices/status and runtime file hashes. It
does not open a PCM stream or change an ALSA control.

## Acceptance gates

The candidate must not be described as protected or Windows-parity complete
until the first boot establishes all of the following:

1. the expected kernel and protected GRUB command line were used;
2. the topology loaded without parser or graph-shape rejection;
3. the integrated graph opened and all six ordered calibration stages
   completed without `SET_CFG`, mapping or media-format error;
4. WSA render and feedback ports allocated without the prior SoundWire clash;
5. both speakers render at deliberately limited gain without loudness cycling;
6. VI/CPS feedback is nonzero, stable and correctly associated with two
   channels.

If any gate fails, preserve `/var/log/sp11-audio-first-boot/latest`, reboot,
and allow the unchanged saved entry `sp11-7.1.5-clean` to load.
