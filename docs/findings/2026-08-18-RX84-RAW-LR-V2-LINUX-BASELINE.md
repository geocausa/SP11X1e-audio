## Why a new oracle was created

The historical fixed-geometry Windows/Linux L/R comparison used the SP7 default
shared capture path and its absolute cross-OS dB values were later demoted to
provisional.  The hardened RAW recorder now also proves and gates the SP7
hardware microphone endpoint gain, so the remaining downstream L/R question can
be repeated without the old capture-engine or microphone-slider ambiguity.

`tools/generate_sp11_lr_raw_v2.py` is the versioned source generator.  It emits:

- 48 kHz stereo PCM16;
- 38.000 s total duration;

- 100 ms cosine ramps;
- frequencies `100,125,160,200,250,315,400,500,630,800,1000,1250,1600,2000,2500,3150,4000,5000,6300` Hz;
- 2 s silence, 10 s left-only, 2 s silence, 10 s right-only,
  2 s silence, 10 s stereo, 2 s silence.

## Extraction method

The coherent tone extractor uses the previously established common SP11->SP7
alignment (`+0.670 s`) and takes six central one-second blocks from every
10-second active segment.  These windows remain far from segment boundaries even
under much larger timing uncertainty; therefore the result does not depend on a
sub-frame onset detector.  Each tone is extracted coherently at its exact known
frequency, median-voted within each run and then compared across the three runs.
